📡 How to get the video URL from your Nextcloud server:
1. Upload your video to Nextcloud and click on it to open.
2. Click the ⋯ Share icon → Share link → enable Allow download.
3. Copy the share link. It looks like: https://cloud.example.com/s/AbCdEfGhIj
4. Add /download at the end → https://cloud.example.com/s/AbCdEfGhIj/download — paste this as your Video URL below.
5. Use the same /download URL for the Download URL too. Your visitors can then stream AND download directly from your server.
