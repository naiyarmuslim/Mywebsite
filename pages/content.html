Why I create content
Baltistan is a remote, mountainous region in northern Pakistan with incredible people and enormous potential — but limited access to quality education in the local language. I create study guide videos in Balti on Facebook so that students back home can learn Math, Science, and Computer concepts in their mother tongue. On Instagram, I share developer life, travel reels, and content to inspire young learners from the region.
