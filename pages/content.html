Why I create content
Baltistan is a remote, mountainous region in northern Pakistan with incredible people and enormous potential — but limited access to verified information. So as a son of this region, I aim to bridge the gap by creating accessible content On Facebook. I create guidance videos in Balti about visa procedure, blocked account, language requirements, visa types, and pathways for diffrent groups of Students. On Instagram, I share developer life, travel reels, and content to inspire the youth that if i can so can they.
