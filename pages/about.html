my story

Developer with a
human-first mindset.

I'm Naiyar Muslim, from Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan — one of the most breathtaking corners of the world. Growing up with a tech enthausiast Dad, i got my hand on computer "a pentium 3 intel System" at a very young age. with no internet access, I taught myself to diagnose hardware problems from first principles: RAM, CPUs, boot configs. Curiosity was my only tool.

Today I'm studying Applied Computer Science in Germany with a C1/DSH-2 German certificate. My core programming languages are C, Java, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Before university I was a teacher, a call-center agent (12-hour night shifts), a kitchen professional, and a delivery helper. Every role sharpened a different skill. I bring that work ethic and adaptability to every codebase and team I join.

📲 Content Creator · Instagram

I also creat instagram reels and informative videos about studentlife in germany in Balti Language for the people of Gilgit-Baltistan — and I'm currently producing Reels for my Hochschule's Pre-college & Pre-study programs. I film, record audio, and edit everything to optimize the video for Social media.
