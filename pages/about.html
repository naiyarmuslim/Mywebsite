my story

Hello! I'm Naiyar Muslim. My journey began in the breathtaking landscapes of Gilgit-Baltistan, a region of towering peaks and rich culture in northern Pakistan. I've always been driven by curiosity — whether it's understanding network architecture, mastering code, or learning a new culinary technique.

Currently I'm an Applied Computer Science student in Germany, studying with a German proficiency of C1 (DSH-2). My technical approach is defined by what I call "Innovative Persistence" — when I want to build something, it doesn't matter if I know how yet. I will learn, and I will do it.

Beyond code, I spent years as a volunteer teacher — teaching English, Math, and Science in Baltistan. I also create study guide videos in Balti on Facebook and lifestyle reels on Instagram, making knowledge accessible to my community back home.
