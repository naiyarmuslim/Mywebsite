my story

Developer with a
human-first mindset.

I'm Naiyar Muslim, from the mountains of Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan — one of the most breathtaking corners of the world. Growing up with little internet access, I taught myself to diagnose hardware problems from first principles: RAM, CPUs, boot configs. Curiosity was my only tool.

Today I'm studying Applied Computer Science in Germany with a C1/DSH-2 German certificate. I build self-hosted cloud servers, web apps, and ML projects — always chasing my goal in Cyber Security and Network Architecture.

Before university I was a teacher, a call-center agent (12-hour night shifts), a kitchen professional, and a delivery helper. Every role sharpened a different skill. I bring that work ethic and adaptability to every codebase and team I join.

📲 Content Creator · Instagram

I create educational content in Balti for the people of Gilgit-Baltistan — and I'm currently producing 1-min interview reels for my Hochschule's Pre-college & Pre-study programs. I film, record audio, and edit everything myself.
